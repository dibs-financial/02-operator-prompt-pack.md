# theresanaiforthat.com scan — 2026-09-21

Question: is there anything on There's An AI For That that helps what we are building (RubyVox phone agent for The Dallas Play House, operated from JetBrains, visits on The Playbook calendar)?

Method: site search via web search restricted to the directory. Direct page fetch was blocked by the session's network policy, so details below come from listing snippets, not from trying the products.

## Worth a look

| Tool | What it does | Why it fits | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apten** | AI SMS agent that books appointments, sends reminders, revives stale leads, and can switch to voice or email | Covers the follow-up text and no-show reminder gap around RubyVox bookings | No public pricing; enterprise-leaning |
| **Rosie** | AI call agent that books, then sends confirmation texts and pre-visit reminder calls | Same no-show reduction, closer to what RubyVox already does | Overlaps RubyVox; only useful if RubyVox lacks reminders |
| **Vozexo** | Voice receptionist that books straight into Google Calendar, 50+ languages | Proof that direct Google Calendar booking from a voice agent is a standard feature to expect from RubyVox | Would be a replacement, not an add-on |
| **Brice** | Email-CC scheduling assistant that proposes times and handles reschedules on Google Calendar | Fits the reschedule-by-email path for visits booked by hand | Email only; no phone or SMS |
| **Cal.ai** | Forward an email, it books or reschedules on your calendar | Same as Brice, cheap | Listed at £18/mo; email only |
| **Toki AI** | Scheduling over WhatsApp plus Google/Apple/Outlook calendars | Useful if callers prefer WhatsApp to SMS | Another inbox to watch |

## Not worth it

- **AI Event Scheduler, Calendar Event Creator, Calendar Link Generator**: Chrome extensions and GPTs that type events faster. JetBrains chat plus the calendar prompts already do this.
- **Leadfwd, Leadodo, ChatSMB**: outbound lead-gen and website chat. Not the problem we are solving.
- **My AI Front Desk, OnCallClerk, AgentVoice**: full receptionist platforms. Same category as RubyVox. Keep as fallback options only.

## Recommendation

1. First check whether RubyVox already sends confirmation and reminder texts after a booking. If it does, nothing from this list is needed now.
2. If it does not, trial **Apten** (SMS-first, appointment-aware) as the reminder and follow-up layer. Rosie is the alternative if a reminder call beats a reminder text for this audience.
3. Keep Brice or Cal.ai in mind only for visits booked by email rather than by phone.
