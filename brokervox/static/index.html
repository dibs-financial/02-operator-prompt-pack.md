What it handles

The broker's job, minus the parts that need a broker.

Everything below is phone calls and paperwork. The desk does them the way a good broker would, and it never sets the rate.

Shipper intake

Takes the load call. Origin, destination, commodity, weight, equipment, pickup and delivery windows, reference. Logged and ready to cover.

Carrier sourcing

Calls carriers on the lane, confirms equipment and availability, and offers the load at the rate you set.

Rate confirmations

Sends the rate con, collects the signature, and files it against the load. Same for the carrier packet.

Check calls and tracking

Calls the driver at pickup, in transit, and at delivery. Location, status, ETA, logged every time.

Delays and exceptions

Detention, breakdowns, missed appointments, rejected freight. Flagged to you the moment they come in, with the context.

Delivery and billing prep

Collects the proof of delivery, matches it to the rate con, and marks the load ready to invoice.

Where the line is drawn

What stays with the broker.

The rate

You set it. The desk carries it to carriers and shippers and never moves it on its own.

The close calls

A carrier that almost qualifies, a shipper who wants an exception. Those come to you with the file attached.

The relationships

The desk works under your name. When a customer wants their broker, the call is handed to you, not held.

Transcript after every call

Who called, what was said, what changed. Two sentences and the full text, on every load.

Texts on your behalf

Confirmations, addresses, reference numbers, and follow-ups as text, drafted for your approval when you want it.

Your brokerage on the line

Shippers and carriers hear your company. Not a vendor, not a bot name.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is BrokerVox a freight brokerage?

No. It works inside your brokerage, under your authority and your name. It does not hold authority, contract freight, or take a margin.

Does it set or negotiate rates?

No. You set the rate and the limits. The desk carries your number to carriers and shippers and brings anything outside the limits back to you.

What is the 10% I keep?

Pricing, the carrier decisions that are close calls, exceptions, and the customers who want to hear from their broker. Everything that is judgment, not phone work.

What happens on a call it cannot handle?

It hands the call to you or your team with the shipper, the load, the carrier, and the transcript attached.

Where does it run?

On RubyVox. You set up and manage the desk there, and BrokerVox supplies the freight brokerage setup.

Who is behind it?

DIBS, in Dallas. BrokerVox is a DIBS product.
