What it handles

Every call a freight desk gets.

The desk works the phone the way your best dispatcher would on a good day, and it never gives a rate.

Origin, destination, commodity, weight, equipment, pickup and delivery windows. Logged and handed to you.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is BrokerVox a freight brokerage or a carrier?

No. BrokerVox is a voice layer for freight brokers. It does not hold freight authority, it does not move loads, and it does not contract with shippers or carriers.

Does the desk quote rates?

No. It captures the lane and the ask, tells the caller you will come back with a number, and sends you the details.

What happens on a call it cannot handle?

It takes a message, tells the caller you will follow up, and sends you the transcript with the context attached.

Who is Joe?

Joe is the live freight desk DIBS runs in Dallas. It is the fastest way to hear what a desk sounds like before you build your own.

Where does the desk run?

On RubyVox. You build and manage the desk there, and BrokerVox supplies the freight setup.

Who is behind it?

DIBS, in Dallas. BrokerVox is a DIBS desk product.
