Desk types

One desk for each kind of broker.

Each desk knows the questions that matter in its trade and the ones it must never answer.

Real estate

Buyer or seller, area, timeline, pre-approval. Books the showing or the listing call.

Mortgage

Purchase or refi, price range, timeline. Books the application call. Never quotes a rate.

Insurance

Line of coverage, renewal date, current carrier. Books the review. Never binds coverage.

Freight

Origin, destination, equipment, pickup window. Hands the load to your dispatcher.

M&A

Buy side or sell side, industry, size range. Books the intro. Nothing else is discussed on the line.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is BrokerVox a brokerage?

No. BrokerVox is a voice layer for brokers. It is not a broker-dealer, RIA, lender, carrier, or insurer, and it does not hold a license in any of those trades.

Does the desk give advice or quote prices?

No. It qualifies the caller and books the meeting. Rates, coverage, valuations, and advice stay with you.

What happens on a call it cannot handle?

It takes a message, tells the caller you will follow up, and sends you the transcript with the context attached.

Who is Joe?

Joe is the live freight desk DIBS runs in Dallas. It is the fastest way to hear what a desk sounds like before you build your own.

Where does the desk run?

On RubyVox. You build and manage the desk there, and BrokerVox supplies the trade-specific setup for brokers.

Who is behind it?

DIBS, in Dallas. BrokerVox is a DIBS desk product.
