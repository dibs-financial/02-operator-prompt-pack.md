FAQ

Straight answers.

Is BrokerVox a carrier, a broker, or a load board?

No. BrokerVox is the communication line between a trucking business and its own trucks. It does not move freight, hold authority, or match loads.

Does it replace dispatch?

No. It takes the phone work off dispatch and writes it down. People still make the decisions.

What happens on a call it cannot handle?

It hands the call to a person on your team, with the driver, the load, and the transcript attached.

Does it work with drivers who are not in the roster?

Yes. It takes the call, asks for name and unit, and flags the unknown number to dispatch.

Where does the line run?

On RubyVox. You set up and manage the line there, and BrokerVox supplies the trucking setup.

Who is behind it?

DIBS, in Dallas. BrokerVox is a DIBS product.
